The Cities: Skylines II mod that exports your city — or let the app install it

Local and personal use. The ripper extracts assets from your own copy of the game so you can use them in software you own; do not publish or redistribute what it produces.

Three steps

  1. Install the ripper mod. Open the app and press Install the ripper mod — it knows where Cities: Skylines II keeps its mods and puts it there. (Or unzip CartoRipper-*.zip into …\AppData\LocalLow\Colossal Order\Cities Skylines II\Mods\ yourself.)
  2. Rip your city. In the game: Options → Carto → Ripper → Rip World. A full city takes about 28 minutes; untick Rip Objects and Rip Terrain for a five-minute roads-only pass. The bundle lands in the game's own ModsData folder, where the app looks for it.
  3. Build the level. Open the app, check it found your bundle and your BeamNG folder, pick a quality preset, press Build level. First build of a full city is a couple of hours — later ones only rebuild what changed. Then start BeamNG and pick your city.
“Windows protected your PC”. The app is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen warns about it the first time. Click More info → Run anyway. Every release also ships a .sha256 file if you would rather check the download yourself.

Prefer the command line?

Everything the app does, cs2beamng-cli does — same settings, same stages, same level. Install it with pipx (no clone, no virtualenv of your own): 

pipx install csii-world-tools
cs2beamng doctor          # what it found on this machine, and what is missing
cs2beamng                 # the app, if you change your mind

Build a command below; every setting has a --set name, and the reference lists all 79.

cs2beamng build

When something is wrong

The app says it found no bundle

Either the mod is not installed, or the rip has not finished — the bundle's manifest (bundle.json) is written last, so a folder full of files without one means a rip is still running or was interrupted. cs2beamng doctor says which.

BeamNG loads the level and the ground is grey / textures are missing

Close BeamNG and build again with Compile shapes after the build on: the level ships pre-compiled meshes and pre-compressed textures, and that stage is skipped while the game is running.

It runs, then gets worse the longer I play

That is the level not fitting in VRAM. Use the performance or medium preset, or build a region instead of the whole city. The app reads your card and resolution out of BeamNG's own log and tells you what a level may mount on it.

Street lights do nothing

BeamNG's Basic Lighting has no depth prepass and shades no point or spot light at all, so every light in the level is pure cost. Switch to Advanced Lighting, or leave lights off.

How it works

The mod walks your loaded city in-engine and writes a documented, engine-neutral CS2 World Bundle: real vertex and index buffers, materials and textures, the terrain heightmap and splats, the lane graph, and a transform and attributes for every instance. The app reads that bundle through its published contract and writes a BeamNG level — terrain, water, sky, roads, markings, buildings, props, 1.2 M placements, an AI navgraph, collision. Neither half knows anything about the other beyond the bundle.

Where the two games disagree, the rule is taken from the game's own decompiled implementation rather than invented; where no rule exists, the approximation is declared and counted in the build report. The bundle schema, a JSON Schema per layer and an example bundle are all in the repository, so another consumer can be written against them without reading a line of the mod.
